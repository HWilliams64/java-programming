Making a team player dependable

Discussion Topic

Making a team player dependable

Discussion Prompt

Choose one boundary where your Ghost player depends on another component: the turn parser, shared file, dictionary, or packaged launch. State what that component promises and what your player must do in return. Describe one ordinary case and one failure or boundary case with an expected result. Explain how you would test the components together with the provided arbiter, and identify one real result or unresolved issue from your current project work.

Context and Purpose

A method can pass an isolated check while the complete player still responds to the wrong turn or launches without its data. Integration checks ask whether the cooperating parts agree on the same protocol and inputs.

Keep your discussion specific to the supplied Ghost rules. You may describe a trace or a test without posting your full solution. Separate behavior you actually observed from behavior you still plan to test.

Requirements and Logistics

  • Describe one component boundary, its promises, and your player’s responsibility.
  • Include an ordinary case and a boundary or failure case with explicit expected behavior.
  • Explain the arbiter check and report one observed result or clearly labeled unresolved issue.
  • Suggest a useful additional test or ask a precise question about a classmate’s boundary.
  • Explain your reasoning without posting a complete team solution; follow the instructor’s discussion schedule.
